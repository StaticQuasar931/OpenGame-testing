Build an original browser-based 3D open-world driving and action game called "Static City Drive".

Requirements:
- Original city map with roads, buildings, sidewalks, streetlights, and props.
- Third-person driving with WASD or arrows.
- Space brake, Shift boost, R reset.
- 10 glowing packages to collect.
- Timer, score, package counter, damage, wanted level, and instructions UI.
- Police chase begins after 3 packages.
- Police contact can defeat the player after multiple hits.
- Add a minimap or guidance arrow to the next package.
- Add a simple weapon or anti-police tool.
- Keep it light and Chromebook-friendly.
- Use static HTML/CSS/JS and make it directly browser-playable.
