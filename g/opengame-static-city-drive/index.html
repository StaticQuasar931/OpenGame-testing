How to Play

Drive around the city and collect all 10 glowing packages. After the third pickup, police cruisers start hunting you down.

  • WASD or arrow keys steer and drive.
  • Space brakes hard for tight corners.
  • Shift gives a short boost burst.
  • F fires an arcade pulse that shoves nearby police away.
  • Three police hits ends the run.
  • R resets your car position if you get wedged.
