Static City Drive: OpenGame Edition

Objective:
Collect all 10 glowing packages across the city grid.

Threat:
After 3 pickups, police cruisers begin chasing you.
Three police hits ends the run.

Controls:
WASD or arrow keys = drive
Space = brake
Shift = boost
F = pulse tool
R = reset car
P = pause

Note:
This folder started as the locally generated OpenGame version and was tuned into a cleaner playable build for direct browser use.
